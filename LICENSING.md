# Diagramble licensing, in short

*This is a summary, not the license. The legal terms are in [LICENSE](LICENSE), and where
this page and the license differ, the license governs.*

The idea: educators use Diagramble freely, and for-profit use pays for its upkeep.

## Free

- Public and non-profit schools, colleges and universities, using and hosting it for
  their own students and staff, including in courses that earn credit.
- Students, instructors and anyone else using it for their own learning, teaching or
  projects, including students at for-profit schools.
- Being paid once to set it up for a non-profit that hosts it itself.
- Answering people's questions about it for free.
- Trying it out, testing it and working on its code.
- Everything you make with it: diagrams, documents and PDFs.

## Needs a paid license

- Any for-profit organization using it, including for-profit schools and companies
  training their own staff.
- Selling it, or bundling it with a textbook, courseware or another product.
- Hosting it for anyone in return for money or any other benefit, even for a non-profit.
- Charging anyone, on an ongoing basis, to run, maintain or support it.
- Companies paid to run a college's courses that use it, such as online program
  managers.
- Paid courses or training that earn no academic credit.

Each version becomes open source under the AGPL four years after it's released.
